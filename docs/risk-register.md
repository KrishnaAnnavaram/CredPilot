# CredPilot — Risk Register

**System:** CredPilot underwriting copilot — multi-product agentic RAG over mortgage and education lending policy.
**Scope of this register:** the retrieval, reasoning and recommendation subsystem in this repository. It does not cover the origination platform CredPilot would sit inside, the servicing system, or the production data pipeline, none of which exist here.
**Status:** pre-production. Every figure below was measured against synthetic data, on a single machine, with no production traffic behind it.
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-21, after the Supervisor, MCP and rule-coverage work.

Categories are OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025) and the NIST AI RMF 1.0 functions (GOVERN / MAP / MEASURE / MANAGE).

Likelihood and impact are scored **Low / Medium / High** for this system as built, not for the general class of risk. Residual risk is what remains *after* the stated mitigation, and is deliberately not zero anywhere.

---

## How to read the residual column

A residual of **Low** means the control is implemented, tested, and the test runs in CI. A residual of **Medium** means the control exists but has a known gap named in the row. A residual of **High** means the risk is accepted and not yet controlled — there are three of those, and they are listed first so they are not buried.

## Prompt injection and untrusted input

| # | Risk | Category | L | I | Mitigation in place | Residual | Owner |
|---|------|----------|---|---|---------------------|----------|-------|
| R-04 | Applicant-supplied free text instructs the system to ignore policy, raise a threshold, or approve the file. | LLM01 Prompt Injection | High | High | `src.guardrails.sanitize` matches 13 patterns and detects all six adversarial packets committed in the corpus. Detected text sets `requires_human_review` and raises risk to HIGH. `src.context.isolate` renders applicant text **last**, inside a `<<<UNTRUSTED_APPLICANT_TEXT` fence, labelled as data. The decision is made by a deterministic rule engine that never reads the text at all, so a successful injection reaches the narrative and nothing else. | Low | Security |
| R-05 | A novel injection phrasing evades all 13 patterns. | LLM01 | Medium | Low | Pattern matching is the second line, not the first. The structural control is that no model participates in the decision: `recommendation_node` reads eligibility, risk and evidence, never applicant prose. An undetected injection can at most produce misleading narrative text on a file, and the narrative is checked against its evidence afterwards. | Low | Security |
| R-06 | Injected text persists in memory and influences a later session. | LLM01, LLM04 Data Poisoning | Medium | Medium | `ShortTermMemory` keeps the applicant trust class on every turn regardless of age — a fact "stated earlier" by an applicant is still applicant-supplied when recalled. `LongTermMemory` refuses to store `decision`, `policy`, `policy_rule`, `threshold` or `credit_score` at all, so the categories worth poisoning cannot be written. | Low | Security |
| R-07 | Injection reaches the system through a **retrieved policy document** rather than applicant text. | LLM01, LLM04 | Low | High | Not controlled by pattern matching — the corpus is trusted input. Controlled structurally: retrieved text is used to supply thresholds to a rule engine and citations to a narrative, and is never executed as instruction. A poisoned corpus is a corpus-integrity problem, addressed by R-16. | Medium | Security |
| R-29 | **A conversational message carries the injection instead of an application document.** The chat surface added a second untrusted input, and it is the one an attacker reaches directly — they can type into it, where they cannot choose what an applicant's letter of explanation says. | LLM01 | High | Medium | `input_guardrails_node` scans the user's message with the same detector as the packet text, and `supervisor_node` routes a message that fired the detector to `HUMAN_REVIEW` **before** any product specialist runs — so an attack reaches no retrieval, no rule engine and no model. Found during this work: the detector missed *"ignore your previous instructions"* because its qualifier list held only articles and not possessives; widened, and covered by `tests/test_supervisor.py` and `tests/test_web_api.py`. The asymmetry is deliberate — an application carrying adversarial text is still assessed and merely routed to a person, because refusing to underwrite a file would let a hostile attachment stop an applicant's file from being read at all. | Low | Security |
| R-30 | **An MCP client the host does not control drives the tools.** The server is independently runnable by anything that speaks the protocol. | LLM06 Excessive Agency, LLM08 | Medium | Medium | Every product-scoped tool requires a product domain and returns `PRODUCT_CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED` rather than searching both corpora; the outcome-table boundary (`OUTCOME_TABLES`) raises regardless of caller; no tool writes anything. Roots are explicitly **not** an authorization mechanism and the server says so in its own response. A client can read policy and compute figures — which is the point — and cannot decide, store or reach applicant data it was not given. `tests/test_mcp_capabilities.py` exercises this against a real server subprocess. | Low | Security |
| R-31 | **MCP sampling moves generation off the approved provider.** A host that served sampling with whatever model was to hand would silently violate the single-provider requirement. | LLM03 Supply Chain, GOVERN 6.1 | Medium | Medium | The host serves sampling through `src/llm.py` — Gemini — and returns the model name so the server can see who answered; the server refuses and discards a response from any model outside the Gemini family. With no key reachable the request is **declined**, never served by something else. Sampling is a compatibility path and normal operation does not use it, asserted by `test_normal_operation_does_not_depend_on_sampling`. | Low | Security |
| R-32 | **Elicitation is used to ask for something it should not.** A protocol that lets a server ask the user a question can be used to ask for a credential. | LLM02, LLM01 | Low | High | The one elicitation tool asks for a lending product and an optional date, against a typed schema with exactly those two fields — there is no free-text field to smuggle a request through. A client with no responder **declines** rather than answering on the user's behalf, and every elicitation is recorded on the client and in `logs/mcp_transcript.jsonl`. Stated as a constraint in `mcp_server/capabilities.py`, not only as a practice. | Low | Security |

## Decision integrity

| # | Risk | Category | L | I | Mitigation in place | Residual | Owner |
|---|------|----------|---|---|---------------------|----------|-------|
| R-12 | A language model produces an underwriting figure — DTI, LTV, reserves — and it is wrong. | LLM09 Misinformation | Low | High | Structurally impossible in the current graph. Every figure comes from `src.calculations`; the model receives them already computed with their formula version. `POL-DTI-001` DTI-CALC-002 forbids the alternative. | Low | Engineering |
| R-13 | The narrative cites a rule that does not support what it claims, or quotes a figure from nowhere. | LLM09, LLM05 Improper Output Handling | Medium | High | `src.narrative.verify_narrative` checks every citation and every figure in the generated prose against the evidence that was supplied, deterministically, after generation. A rationale that fails is **kept, marked unfaithful, and routed to a human** — not silently dropped, which would hide the failure, and not shipped unmarked, which would be worse. | Low | AI Evaluation |
| R-14 | Evidence from the wrong product reaches a decision — education policy applied to a mortgage. | LLM08 Vector and Embedding Weaknesses | Low | High | Product isolation is structural, not a filter: separate Chroma collections (`credpilot_mortgage_policies`, `credpilot_education_policies`) resolved before retrieval runs. There is no combined collection to leak from. `cross_product_contamination_rate` is measured at **0.00**. Where the product cannot be resolved the graph escalates rather than searching both. | Low | Retrieval |
| R-15 | A superseded policy version is applied to a file whose as-of date predates the change. | LLM09, MEASURE 2.11 | Medium | High | Effective-date windows are evaluated before ranking, and the latest *eligible* version is selected per the file's as-of date. The `APP-000055/56/57` boundary triple exercises both sides of a version change and the day it lands. | Low | Retrieval |
| R-16 | The indexed corpus drifts from the committed policy documents. | LLM04 Data Poisoning, MANAGE 4.1 | Medium | High | `index_manifest.json` records a content hash per source document; `src.rag.integrity` runs 16 checks including hash agreement, and `scripts/check_published_figures.py` plus `scripts/verify_doc_tables.py` run as tests so a documented figure cannot silently diverge from the measured one. | Low | Retrieval |
| R-27 | **The rule engine does not evaluate every declared rule family, so a file the policy says a human must see can be approved instead.** Measured before this work: 33 of 75 mortgage and 17 of 20 education golden cases turned on a family the engine never compared against anything. | LLM06 Excessive Agency, MEASURE 2.3 | Medium | High | **Substantially closed, and the remainder published rather than absorbed.** Eleven families were implemented — mortgage `GEN-ELG`, `DOC-REQ`, `EMP-CNT`, `CRD-EVT`, `CRD-DLQ`, `AST-SRC`, `VAL-APR`, `JMB-ELG`; education `EDU-UW`, `EDU-RG`, `EDU-COS`, `EDU-INTL` — taking the figure to **15 of 75 mortgage (20%)** and **0 of 20 education (0%)**. Each reads its thresholds from the retrieved rule and reports INDETERMINATE when that rule is absent, so a retrieval regression surfaces as a referral rather than an approval (`tests/test_rule_families.py` asserts this per family). Retrieval was extended alongside: a required-rule pass fetches by id whatever the topic pass did not land, because an implemented rule the retriever misses is worth nothing (F-17). What remains is a long tail of single-case families — `PRP-ELG`, `INC-*`, `KYC-IDV`, `TTL-LIE`, the government overlays — listed with their case counts in `rule_coverage`. | **Medium** | Credit Policy / Engineering |
| R-17 | Missing evidence is treated as evidence of ineligibility, producing a wrongful decline. | LLM09, GOVERN 1.1 | Medium | High | Found in testing as failure F-8: `DTI-CONV-003` was not retrieved, the engine read "0 of 2 compensating factors documented" and declined `APP-000057`. Fixed twice over — the retrieval agent now follows rule references it cannot resolve (up to 6), and the rule engine returns `INDETERMINATE` rather than `FAIL` on absent evidence, which routes to referral. `GEN-ELG-005` states the principle. | Low | Engineering |

## Agency and consumption

| # | Risk | Category | L | I | Mitigation in place | Residual | Owner |
|---|------|----------|---|---|---------------------|----------|-------|
| R-18 | The system issues a binding credit decision without a human. | LLM06 Excessive Agency, GOVERN 1.1 | Low | High | The system produces a **recommendation**, never a decision — the outcome vocabulary is `APPROVE_RECOMMENDATION` / `REFER_RECOMMENDATION` / `DECLINE_RECOMMENDATION`. A decline is always routed to a human (`POL-DEC-001` DEC-REC-002), as is any indeterminate result, any HIGH risk level, and any file where applicant text attempted instruction. | Low | Credit Policy |
| R-19 | An agent loop runs unbounded against one file, spending without reaching a decision. | LLM10 Unbounded Consumption | Low | Medium | Two independent guards, because they fail differently. An in-state `step_budget` of 32 lets a node see the budget running out and **halt cleanly with a reason a human can read**; LangGraph's `recursion_limit` of 60 is the external backstop for a cycle that never reaches a node able to check anything. A normal assessment uses 11 and a clarified conversation 13. A third bound closes the one cycle the graph actually contains: `clarification → supervisor → clarification` is capped at `MAX_CLARIFICATION_ROUNDS = 2`, after which the thread goes to a person rather than being asked a third time. | Low | Engineering |
| R-33 | **An external call hangs and the file never comes back.** Retrieval, the model and every MCP call cross a boundary that can stop answering. | LLM10, MANAGE 2.3 | Medium | Medium | `src.resilience` puts a deadline and a bounded attempt count on every one. Retries are capped and the count is reported; a fault that will recur identically — a malformed response, a validation error, a refusal — raises `NonRetryableError` and stops after one attempt rather than spending three round trips on the same answer. Failures come back as a `ToolFailure` **value**, so a graph node degrades and records the loss instead of dying inside a checkpoint. A file that lost one of fifteen retrievals is still assessed with the gap reported; one that lost all of them is referred, never approved. Covered by `tests/test_resilience.py`. | Low | Engineering |
| R-20 | Reasoning tokens inflate cost invisibly — billed as output, never present in the text. | LLM10 | Medium | Low | Measured rather than assumed, and the measurement belongs to the prompt rather than the model: a short under-specified probe drew 388 of 419 output tokens as reasoning, while the actual narrative prompt — long, fully specified, decision already made — draws **zero**, output matching visible text. `thinking_budget=0` is accepted and ignored; `thinking_level="low"` is set and does move it where the model thinks at all. `reasoning_tokens` is reported as its own line in `usage_of()` and `reports/golden_signals.json`, so a future prompt change that starts drawing reasoning shows up as a figure rather than as a bill. | Low | Engineering |
| R-28 | **The checkpoint store grows without bound.** Every node writes its state, including the full retrieved evidence, and nothing prunes. Observed directly, and re-measured as this work finished: `credpilot_checkpoints.sqlite` reached **793 MB** across **622 threads** and 7,864 checkpoint rows — about 1.3 MB per run, and it was 537 MB a day earlier. The growth is linear in runs, which is what makes it a capacity question rather than a leak. | LLM10 Unbounded Consumption, MANAGE 2.3 | High | Medium | **Not controlled.** The directory is git-ignored so nothing is committed, and a single assessment is unaffected, but at this rate a production deployment fills a disk in weeks and the failure arrives as a write error mid-assessment rather than as a warning. A retention policy on the checkpointer — prune completed threads past a window — is the fix and is not written. | **Medium** | Engineering |
| R-21 | Tool errors or provider outages fail a file rather than degrading it. | MANAGE 2.3 | Medium | Medium | The narrative node degrades to a deterministic summary when Gemini is unreachable and marks the result `available: false`; a recommendation without prose is still a recommendation. Retrieval, indexing, the rule engine, the tests and the retrieval evaluation need no model at all and run unchanged. | Low | Engineering |

## Summary

| Residual | Count | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| High | 3 | R-01, R-02, R-03 |
| Medium | 6 | R-07, R-10, R-25, R-26, R-27, R-28 |
| Low | 24 | the remainder |

All three remaining High rows share a cause: **this system has been verified against itself.** The data is synthetic, the judge is the same model family as the generator of the prose it grades, and no credit professional has checked that the policy corpus says the right things. No amount of further engineering inside this repository will close them.

R-27 was the fourth, and was the one that *was* closable here. It has been: eleven rule families were implemented, taking the share of golden cases turning on an unevaluated family from 44% to 20% for mortgage and from 85% to zero for education. It stays at Medium rather than Low because a tail of single-case families remains, and because the families now implemented are only as good as the retrieval that feeds them — which is why the required-rule pass was added in the same change and why `tests/test_rule_families.py` asserts that a missing rule produces INDETERMINATE rather than a pass.

The three that remain should be the first items any production readiness review takes up. None of them will be closed by further engineering inside this repository.

**Review cadence:** on any change to the policy corpus, the model or provider, the guardrail patterns, or the evaluation harness; and quarterly otherwise.
