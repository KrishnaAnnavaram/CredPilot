# POL-012: International Student Lending Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the eligibility criteria, documentation requirements, risk adjustments, and underwriting rules specific to Edgemont Education Lending's International Student (INTL) loan product. International student lending carries unique risks related to visa status, post-graduation employment authorization, cross-border collections, and limited U.S. credit history. This policy supplements the general underwriting standards in POL-001 through POL-006 and must be read in conjunction with those policies.

## 2. Eligible Visa Types

**EDU-INTL-001 — Visa Eligibility**

Only applicants holding one of the following non-immigrant visa classifications are eligible for the INTL loan product:

- **F-1** — Academic student visa, issued to individuals enrolled in a full course of study at a Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP)-certified academic institution.
- **J-1** — Exchange visitor visa, issued to individuals participating in an approved exchange visitor program, provided the program is degree-granting or leads to a recognized credential.

All other visa types — including but not limited to H-1B, L-1, B-1/B-2, O-1, and TN — are **not eligible** for the INTL product. Holders of other work-authorized visas who are pursuing education may apply under the GR or SP products if they meet the standard eligibility criteria (U.S. residency, SSN/ITIN, etc.).

## 3. Immigration Document Verification

**EDU-INTL-002 — I-20 and DS-2019 Verification**

The following immigration documents must be verified and retained in the loan file:

- **F-1 visa holders** must present a valid, unexpired **Form I-20** (Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status) issued by the applicant's designated school official (DSO). The I-20 must reflect the applicant's current program, expected completion date, and SEVIS ID.
- **J-1 visa holders** must present a valid, unexpired **Form DS-2019** (Certificate of Eligibility for Exchange Visitor Status) issued by the applicant's responsible officer (RO). The DS-2019 must reflect the current program and dates of participation.

Documents are verified against SEVIS records where access is available, or through direct confirmation with the school's international student office.

**EDU-INTL-003 — I-94 Verification**

The applicant's **Form I-94** (Arrival/Departure Record) must be obtained and verified to confirm lawful admission and current status. The I-94 is retrieved electronically from the CBP I-94 website using the applicant's passport and travel information.

- If the I-94 is **successfully verified** and confirms valid F-1 or J-1 status with a "D/S" (duration of status) notation, the application proceeds.
- If the I-94 **cannot be verified** — due to system unavailability, data mismatch, or absence of a retrievable record — the application is assigned a disposition of **REFER_MANUAL**. An underwriter must conduct additional verification (e.g., contacting the school's DSO/RO, requesting a physical I-94 printout, or reviewing passport entry stamps) before the application can proceed.

## 4. No-Cosigner Eligibility (INTL Without Cosigner)

**EDU-INTL-004 — No-Cosigner Pathway Requirements**

International students may apply without a U.S.-based cosigner only if **all** of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. **School tier** — the applicant's institution is classified as **Tier A or Tier B** on Edgemont Education Lending's approved school list (see POL-005 for tier definitions and methodology).
2. **OPT eligibility** — the applicant must be eligible for Optional Practical Training (OPT) upon program completion. For STEM-designated programs, eligibility for the 24-month STEM OPT extension is considered a positive factor but is not required.
3. **Post-study work authorization** — the applicant must have a reasonable expectation of at least **12 months** of post-study work authorization remaining after the expected graduation date. This is assessed based on OPT eligibility, STEM designation, and program completion timeline.
4. All other standard INTL underwriting criteria (credit, income, enrollment) must be met.

Applicants who do not meet all four conditions must provide a qualifying cosigner.

## 5. Cosigner Requirements for INTL Loans

**EDU-INTL-005 — Cosigner Citizenship and Residency**

When a cosigner is required (or voluntarily provided) on an INTL loan, the cosigner must be a **U.S. citizen** or **U.S. permanent resident** (green card holder). The cosigner must reside in the United States and must meet all cosigner credit, income, and eligibility requirements as defined in the applicable product policy (POL-003).

Non-U.S.-resident cosigners and cosigners holding non-immigrant visas are not eligible to serve as cosigners on INTL loans. This requirement reflects the enforceability and collections considerations associated with cross-border obligations.

## 6. Home Country Credit History

**EDU-INTL-006 — International Credit Bureau Data**

Edgemont Education Lending accepts **home country credit reports** as supplementary credit information when available through international credit reporting partnerships. Home country credit data may be used to:

- Supplement a thin or absent U.S. credit file.
- Provide evidence of positive repayment history in the applicant's country of origin.
- Identify derogatory credit events (defaults, insolvency proceedings) in the home country.

Home country credit data is considered on a best-efforts basis and does not replace U.S. credit bureau data where available. If the applicant has both U.S. and home country credit data, both are reviewed. The absence of home country credit data does not, by itself, result in a negative underwriting outcome.

## 7. Sponsor and External Funding Documentation

**EDU-INTL-007 — Financial Sponsorship Verification**

If the applicant intends to use funds from a sponsor (e.g., family member, government scholarship, employer, or non-governmental organization) to support loan repayment or demonstrate financial capacity, the following documentation is required:

1. A **signed sponsorship letter** from the sponsor, stating the amount and duration of financial support.
2. **Bank statements** or other financial documentation from the sponsor demonstrating the ability to provide the stated support (typically the most recent 3 months).
3. If the sponsor is located outside the United States, documents must be translated into English by a certified translator and currency amounts must be converted to U.S. dollars at the prevailing exchange rate.

Sponsor funds may be considered as supplementary support in the affordability assessment but may not serve as the sole basis for meeting income or repayment capacity requirements.

## 8. Cross-Border Collections Risk Adjustment

**EDU-INTL-008 — Risk Premium for Cross-Border Exposure**

To compensate for the elevated risk associated with cross-border collections — including jurisdictional limitations, foreign judgment enforcement challenges, and currency conversion risk — all INTL loans carry a risk adjustment of **+0.25%** (25 basis points) added to the base interest rate that would otherwise apply based on the borrower's credit profile and product tier.

This adjustment applies regardless of whether the borrower has a cosigner. It is disclosed transparently in the Approval Disclosure and Final Disclosure as required by POL-010 (Regulation Z Disclosures).

## 9. Visa Expiration and Post-Graduation Work Authorization

**EDU-INTL-009 — Visa Expiration Threshold**

At the time of credit decision, the system evaluates the applicant's visa status and expected post-graduation work authorization timeline. If the applicant's visa or authorized stay is set to expire within **less than 6 months after the expected graduation date** and the applicant does **not** have approved or pending OPT authorization, the application is **declined** with reason code **RC-015** (Insufficient post-study work authorization).

This rule reflects the risk that the borrower may be required to depart the United States before entering repayment, significantly reducing the probability of successful loan repayment. The 6-month threshold provides a buffer for OPT application processing timelines.

Applicants declined under this rule may reapply once they have obtained OPT approval or an alternative valid work authorization extending at least 12 months beyond the graduation date.

## 10. Interaction with Other Policies

All INTL loans are also subject to:

- **POL-007** — Exception and Override Policy (no exception for OFAC, enrollment fraud, or regulatory prohibition).
- **POL-008** — Fraud, Identity, and AML Policy (CIP requirements apply with acceptable INTL document types).
- **POL-009** — Adverse Action and Reason Codes (INTL-specific codes RC-014, RC-015 available).
- **POL-010** — Regulation Z Disclosures (all disclosure requirements apply).
- **POL-011** — Fair Lending and ECOA (citizenship used only for visa verification, not as a credit factor).

## 11. Effective Date

This policy is effective as of the date in the frontmatter and is reviewed at least annually or upon material changes to immigration regulations or OPT program rules.
