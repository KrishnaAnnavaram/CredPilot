# GOLD-02: UG Decline - No Cosigner, Thin Credit File

## Decision

**DECLINE** | Risk Grade: **E1** | Adverse Action Required: **Yes**

## Reasoning Chain

1. **Borrower eligibility**: Hugo Vasquez, 21-year-old US citizen, meets basic age and citizenship requirements for UG product.

2. **Credit evaluation**: Borrower has NO_FILE credit status -- no FICO score available, no tradelines, no credit history. This is a thin-file applicant who cannot be scored under standard credit evaluation per POL-002 EDU-UW-001.

3. **Cosigner assessment**: No cosigner provided. Under UG policy, borrowers without an established credit file are required to have a qualified cosigner. RC-011 (cosigner required but not provided) applies per POL-009.

4. **Income verification**: No income verification documents submitted. For a student borrower without credit history, income from a cosigner is typically relied upon.

5. **School certification**: Maplewood College is Tier B, regionally accredited, and on the approved list. School eligibility is not the issue.

6. **Risk grade**: E1 assigned due to unscoreable borrower without cosigner support, representing maximum risk classification.

## Policy Citations

- POL-002 EDU-UW-001 (underwriting standards -- credit file requirements)
- POL-009 RC-002 (insufficient credit history)
- POL-009 RC-011 (cosigner required but not provided)

## Documents Requested

None. The issue is structural -- a cosigner must be added to the application.

## Conditions

None. Hard decline. Borrower should be advised to reapply with a qualified cosigner who meets citizenship and credit requirements per POL-004 EDU-COS-001.
