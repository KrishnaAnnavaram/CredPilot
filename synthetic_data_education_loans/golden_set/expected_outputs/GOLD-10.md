# GOLD-10: INTL Decline Due to Visa/Immigration Eligibility Issues

## Decision

**DECLINE** | Risk Grade: **E1** | Adverse Action Required: **Yes**

## Reasoning Chain

1. **Borrower eligibility**: Xander Zhou, 28-year-old F-1 visa holder from South Korea. Applying for INTL product.

2. **Immigration concerns**: While the borrower has a valid F-1 visa with I-94 verified, several factors trigger RC-015 (visa/immigration eligibility):
   - Not STEM OPT eligible, limiting post-study work authorization to only 12 months
   - Sponsor funds NOT documented, failing a key INTL requirement
   - Limited post-study work period creates significant repayment risk

3. **School risk**: Pinnacle Online University is Tier D with 16% CDR and only 48% completion rate. DEAC accreditation (national, not regional) is a weaker credential. The combination of a Tier D school and limited post-study work authorization is disqualifying per POL-010 EDU-INTL-001.

4. **Credit evaluation**: NO_FILE credit status. No income verification. No cosigner. Without any mitigating factors (strong school, STEM OPT, documented sponsor), the application cannot be supported.

5. **Aggregate exposure**: Prior balance with us of $45,557 adds to concentration risk. Debt-to-projected-income ratio of 3.11 is excessive.

## Policy Citations

- POL-010 EDU-INTL-001 (international student underwriting)
- POL-009 RC-015 (visa/immigration eligibility)
- POL-006 EDU-SCH-001 (school eligibility -- Tier D)

## Documents Requested

None. Hard decline.

None. Application does not meet INTL product eligibility requirements due to insufficient post-study work authorization and undocumented sponsor funds at a Tier D institution.
