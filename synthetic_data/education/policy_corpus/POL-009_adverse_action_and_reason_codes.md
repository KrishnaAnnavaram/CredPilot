# POL-009: Adverse Action and Reason Code Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs Edgemont Education Lending's obligations when taking adverse action on a private education loan application, including denials, counteroffers, and approved-with-conditions decisions across all products (UG, GR, SP, INTL, REFI). It ensures compliance with the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA), Regulation B (12 CFR 1002), and the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA).

## 2. Adverse Action Notice Requirements

**EDU-AA-001 — Notice Obligations**

An adverse action notice is required whenever Edgemont Education Lending:

- **Declines** an application (disposition = DECLINE).
- **Approves with conditions** that are materially less favorable than the terms originally requested or advertised (disposition = AWC), including a higher interest rate, reduced loan amount, or requirement of a cosigner not initially contemplated.
- **Revokes** a previously issued approval prior to disbursement.

The adverse action notice must be delivered to the applicant within **30 calendar days** of the adverse action decision, as required by ECOA and Regulation B. Notices may be delivered electronically if the applicant has provided valid E-SIGN consent; otherwise, a paper notice must be mailed to the applicant's address of record.

The notice must include, at minimum:

1. The specific action taken (denial, counteroffer, or revocation).
2. The principal reason(s) for the action (see Section 3).
3. The applicant's right to request a copy of the appraisal or valuation (if applicable).
4. The name and contact information of the creditor.
5. The ECOA notice regarding the prohibition of discrimination.
6. If a credit report was used, the name, address, and telephone number of the consumer reporting agency, along with the applicant's right to obtain a free copy of their report within 60 days and to dispute its accuracy.

## 3. Reason Code Requirements

**EDU-AA-002 — Reason Code Selection and Ranking**

Each adverse action notice must include the **principal reasons** for the adverse decision. Edgemont Education Lending provides a **maximum of 4 reason codes**, selected and ranked by their relative impact on the decision:

- Reason codes are ranked from most impactful (Reason 1) to least impactful (Reason 4).
- The ranking must reflect the actual decisioning logic — the factor with the greatest negative weight or the largest gap from the approval threshold appears first.
- If fewer than 4 factors contributed to the adverse decision, only the relevant codes are included.
- Generic or catch-all reason codes (e.g., "other") are prohibited.

### Code Usage Notes

- **RC-018 (OFAC match)** always appears as Reason 1 when present, regardless of other factors.
- **RC-019 (Suspected fraud)** always appears as Reason 1 or 2 when present.
- **RC-021 (Existing loan in default)** applies only to the REFI product; an existing defaulted loan being refinanced is evaluated separately from general delinquency (RC-004).
- **RC-008 (Employment tenure)** does not apply to UG or INTL products where borrowers are typically students without extended employment history.
- **RC-023 (Residual income)** applies only to products where residual income is a decisioning factor (GR, SP, REFI).

## 5. Risk-Based Pricing Notices

**EDU-AA-004 — FCRA Risk-Based Pricing Notice**

When an applicant is approved but the offered interest rate exceeds the **best-available rate** for the same product and term by more than **200 basis points (2.00%)**, Edgemont Education Lending must provide a risk-based pricing notice in compliance with the Fair Credit Reporting Act and Regulation V (12 CFR 1022).

The risk-based pricing notice must include:

1. A statement that the terms offered are based in whole or in part on information from a consumer report.
2. The identity of each consumer reporting agency that furnished a report used in the decision.
3. The applicant's right to obtain a free copy of the report and to dispute inaccurate information.
4. The applicant's credit score, the range of possible scores, and the key factors adversely affecting the score.

As an alternative, Edgemont Education Lending may elect to use the **credit score disclosure exception** permitted under 12 CFR 1022.74(d), providing a credit score disclosure to all applicants at the time of the credit decision in lieu of individual risk-based pricing notices.

## 6. Record Retention

All adverse action notices, reason code selections, and risk-based pricing notices must be retained for a minimum of **25 months** from the date of the action, consistent with Regulation B requirements. Electronic records are acceptable provided they are readily retrievable for examination purposes.

## 7. Effective Date

This policy is effective as of the date in the frontmatter and is reviewed at least annually or upon regulatory change.
