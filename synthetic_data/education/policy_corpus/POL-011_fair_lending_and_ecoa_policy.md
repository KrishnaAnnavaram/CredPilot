# POL-011: Fair Lending and Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Edgemont Education Lending's commitment to fair, equitable, and non-discriminatory lending practices across all private education loan products (UG, GR, SP, INTL, REFI). It ensures compliance with the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA, 15 U.S.C. 1691 et seq.), Regulation B (12 CFR 1002), the Fair Housing Act (where applicable), and all relevant state fair lending statutes. Edgemont Education Lending is dedicated to evaluating every applicant on individual creditworthiness and eligibility criteria, without regard to any prohibited basis.

## 2. Prohibited Bases for Discrimination

**EDU-FL-001 — Protected Characteristics**

No employee, agent, contractor, or automated system of Edgemont Education Lending may consider, directly or indirectly, any of the following prohibited bases when evaluating a credit application, setting loan terms, or taking any other credit action:

- **Race** or color
- **Religion**
- **National origin**
- **Sex** (including gender identity and sexual orientation)
- **Marital status**
- **Age** (provided the applicant has the legal capacity to contract; see Section 3)
- **Receipt of income from any public assistance program**

Additionally, Edgemont Education Lending prohibits discrimination based on the applicant's good-faith exercise of any right under the Consumer Credit Protection Act. These prohibitions apply to every stage of the credit lifecycle: marketing, application intake, underwriting, pricing, servicing, collection, and default management.

## 3. Age of Majority and Minor Applicants

**EDU-FL-002 — Age Requirements**

The minimum age for a borrower is **18 years old** at the time of application, consistent with the legal age of majority in most U.S. jurisdictions. Applicants who are **17 years old** may be eligible in states where the age of majority is 17 or where state law permits minors to enter into enforceable education loan contracts, **provided** that a qualifying cosigner is included on the application.

Age may be used as a factor only to the extent necessary to determine:

1. Whether the applicant has the legal capacity to enter into a binding contract.
2. The applicant's eligibility for age-specific protections (e.g., the CARD Act provisions applicable to borrowers under 21, where relevant).

Age may not be used as a negative factor in credit scoring or underwriting beyond the capacity determination. An elderly applicant may not be disadvantaged due to age-related assumptions about income longevity or mortality.

## 4. Disparate Impact Testing

**EDU-FL-003 — Quarterly Statistical Analysis**

Edgemont Education Lending conducts **quarterly disparate impact testing** across all products to identify any statistically significant differences in approval rates, pricing outcomes, or exception frequency among applicant groups defined by prohibited characteristics.

The testing program includes:

1. **Approval rate analysis** — comparison of approval/denial rates across demographic groups, controlling for legitimate credit factors.
2. **Pricing analysis** — evaluation of interest rate spreads and fee assessments to detect unexplained disparities.
3. **Exception analysis** — review of exception and override activity (see POL-007) to ensure no pattern of preferential treatment based on prohibited characteristics.
4. **Regression modeling** — multivariate regression analysis isolating the effect of demographic factors from creditworthiness variables.

Results are reported to the Fair Lending Officer, the Chief Compliance Officer, and the Board Risk Committee. Any identified disparity that cannot be explained by legitimate, non-discriminatory credit factors triggers a corrective action plan within 60 days.

## 5. Citizenship and Visa Status

**EDU-FL-004 — Permissible Use of Citizenship Information**

Citizenship or immigration status may be considered **only** for the INTL product line, and **only** to the extent necessary to verify that the applicant holds a qualifying visa (F-1 or J-1) as required by POL-012 (International Lending Policy).

For all other products (UG, GR, SP, REFI), citizenship or immigration status may not be used as a factor in the credit decision, provided the applicant has a valid SSN or ITIN and meets all other eligibility requirements. An applicant's national origin may never be used as a proxy for creditworthiness, regardless of product.

## 6. Public Assistance Income

**EDU-FL-005 — Equal Treatment of Public Assistance Income**

Income derived from public assistance programs — including but not limited to Supplemental Security Income (SSI), Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI), Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF), veterans' benefits, and housing assistance — must be treated **equally** with income from employment, self-employment, or other non-public-assistance sources in all underwriting calculations.

Specifically:

- Public assistance income may not be discounted, excluded, or weighted differently from other income sources when calculating debt-to-income ratios or residual income.
- The source of income may not be used as a negative factor or trigger additional scrutiny beyond standard verification procedures.
- Verification of public assistance income follows the same documentation standards as other income types (e.g., award letters, benefit statements, bank deposit records).

## 7. Monitoring Data Segregation

**EDU-FL-006 — Separation of Demographic and Decisioning Data**

Demographic monitoring data collected for fair lending analysis and regulatory reporting — including race, ethnicity, sex, and age — must be **segregated from the underwriting and decisioning workflow**. Specifically:

- Monitoring data is collected on a voluntary basis during the application process.
- The data is stored in a separate database table or access-restricted field that is not visible to underwriters, loan officers, or automated decisioning engines at any point during the credit evaluation.
- Only the Fair Lending Officer, the Compliance team, and authorized analytics personnel have access to monitoring data, and only for statistical analysis and regulatory reporting purposes.
- Monitoring data may never be transmitted to, or used as an input in, any credit scoring model, pricing algorithm, or underwriting rules engine.

## 8. Cosigner and Spousal Protections

**EDU-FL-007 — Prohibition Against Requiring Spouse as Cosigner**

Edgemont Education Lending may **not** require the applicant's spouse to serve as a cosigner if the applicant independently meets all credit, income, and eligibility requirements for the requested product and loan amount. This prohibition applies regardless of the applicant's marital status, the state of residence, or whether the couple resides in a community property state.

If a cosigner is required because the borrower does not independently qualify (e.g., insufficient income or credit score), the borrower may select any eligible individual as cosigner — the cosigner need not be the borrower's spouse, parent, or family member. The cosigner must meet the applicable credit and eligibility requirements defined in the relevant product policy (POL-001 through POL-006).

Edgemont Education Lending may not:

- Inquire about the applicant's marital status except to determine the number of obligors or for state-specific legal capacity purposes (using only the terms "married," "unmarried," or "separated").
- Discount or refuse to consider income from a non-applicant spouse if the borrower voluntarily presents it for qualification purposes.
- Require a spouse's signature on any loan document unless the spouse is a co-borrower or cosigner by choice.

## 9. Training and Accountability

All employees involved in marketing, origination, underwriting, servicing, and collections must complete fair lending training upon hire and annually thereafter. Training records are maintained by the Compliance department. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, and may expose the institution to regulatory enforcement and civil liability.

## 10. Effective Date

This policy is effective as of the date in the frontmatter and is reviewed at least annually or upon regulatory change.
