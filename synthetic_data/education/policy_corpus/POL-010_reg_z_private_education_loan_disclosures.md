# POL-010: Regulation Z Private Education Loan Disclosure Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the disclosure requirements for Edgemont Education Lending's private education loan products (UG, GR, SP, INTL, REFI) in compliance with the Truth in Lending Act (TILA) and Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026), Subpart F — Special Rules for Private Education Loans. These requirements ensure that borrowers receive timely, accurate, and complete information at each stage of the loan process to support informed decision-making.

## 2. Three-Disclosure Sequence

**EDU-DIS-001 — Mandatory Disclosure Stages**

Edgemont Education Lending must provide disclosures at three distinct stages in the private education loan origination process. No stage may be omitted, and each must be delivered in the prescribed form and timing:

1. **Application Disclosure** — provided at the time the application is received or, for applications received electronically, before the applicant submits the application. This disclosure presents the range of rates and terms available for the requested product, general loan features, and borrower rights. It must be clearly labeled as an estimate and not a commitment.

2. **Approval Disclosure** — provided when the loan is approved and the specific terms are set. This disclosure reflects the actual rates, fees, and terms applicable to the individual borrower based on the credit evaluation. It constitutes the formal loan offer.

3. **Final (Consummation) Disclosure** — provided at or before loan consummation. This disclosure confirms the final terms of the loan. If any material term has changed from the Approval Disclosure, the Final Disclosure must clearly identify the change and the borrower must be given a new acceptance and cancellation period.

Each disclosure must be provided in writing (paper or electronic with valid E-SIGN consent) and must be clear and conspicuous as required by Regulation Z.

## 3. Acceptance Window

**EDU-DIS-002 — 30-Day Acceptance Period**

Upon delivery of the Approval Disclosure, the borrower has a minimum of **30 calendar days** to accept the terms of the loan. Edgemont Education Lending may not impose a shorter acceptance window. If the borrower does not accept within the 30-day period, the approval expires and the borrower must reapply or request a new credit evaluation.

During the acceptance period:

- The borrower may accept, reject, or request modification of the offered terms.
- Edgemont Education Lending may not disburse funds until the borrower has affirmatively accepted.
- The borrower's acceptance must be documented in the loan file (electronic signature, signed acceptance form, or recorded verbal acceptance with contemporaneous written confirmation).

## 4. Right to Cancel

**EDU-DIS-003 — 3-Business-Day Cancellation Right**

The borrower has the right to cancel the loan, without penalty, within **3 business days** following the date on which the loan is consummated (i.e., the date the borrower signs the final loan agreement). This right must be conspicuously disclosed in both the Approval Disclosure and the Final Disclosure.

If the borrower exercises the right to cancel:

- All loan documents are voided.
- Any disbursed funds must be returned to Edgemont Education Lending by the school or borrower.
- No fees, interest, or charges may be assessed for the cancelled loan.
- The cancellation must be processed within 5 business days of receipt of the borrower's notice.

## 5. School Certification Sequencing

**EDU-DIS-004 — School Certification Before Approval Disclosure**

The school certification — confirming the borrower's enrollment status, cost of attendance, estimated financial assistance, and the certified loan amount — must be obtained **before** the Approval Disclosure is delivered to the borrower. This ensures that the approved loan amount reflected in the Approval Disclosure does not exceed the school-certified maximum.

For REFI products where school certification is not applicable (the borrower has already completed or separated from the institution), this requirement is waived. A notation must be recorded in the loan file confirming the REFI exemption.

## 6. Rate Lock Period

**EDU-DIS-005 — Minimum Rate Lock Duration**

Once the Approval Disclosure is issued, the disclosed interest rate (whether fixed or the initial rate for a variable-rate loan) must be locked for a minimum of **30 calendar days**. The rate lock period runs concurrently with the acceptance window.

If the borrower accepts the loan after the rate lock has expired but before the approval itself expires, Edgemont Education Lending must either (a) honor the original locked rate or (b) re-disclose with the updated rate and provide a new 30-day acceptance window and 3-day cancellation right.

Rate locks apply to all products. For variable-rate products, the lock applies to the margin and the index value used to calculate the initial rate disclosed on the Approval Disclosure.

## 7. Required Disclosure Content

**EDU-DIS-006 — Mandatory Disclosure Elements**

Each Approval Disclosure and Final Disclosure must contain at least the following information, presented clearly and in a format consistent with Regulation Z model forms:

1. **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)** — expressed as a single annualized rate reflecting interest and applicable fees. For variable-rate loans, the disclosure must include the current APR, the index, the margin, the maximum APR over the life of the loan, and an example of what the monthly payment would be at the maximum rate.
2. **Total loan cost** — the total of all payments the borrower will make over the life of the loan, assuming all payments are made as scheduled.
3. **Payment schedule** — the number, amount, and timing of payments. If the loan features an in-school deferment or grace period, the disclosure must describe the payment terms during and after those periods.
4. **Late fees** — the amount or formula for calculating late payment charges, the grace period after which a payment is considered late, and any caps on late fees.
5. **Prepayment terms** — a statement that the borrower may prepay the loan at any time without penalty. Edgemont Education Lending does not charge prepayment penalties on any product.
6. **Interest accrual during non-payment periods** — a clear statement of whether and how interest accrues during in-school, grace, deferment, and forbearance periods, and the impact of capitalized interest on the total loan cost.

## 8. Cosigner Notice

**EDU-DIS-007 — Cosigner Self-Certification and Notice**

For any loan involving a cosigner, Edgemont Education Lending must provide the cosigner with a separate notice in compliance with 15 U.S.C. 1638(e)(10) that includes:

1. A statement that the cosigner may be required to repay the full amount of the loan if the borrower does not pay.
2. A description of the cosigner's obligations, including that the cosigner's credit may be adversely affected if the borrower fails to make timely payments.
3. Notification that the cosigner may be subject to collection activity, including wage garnishment and litigation, if the loan enters default.
4. Information about any cosigner release provisions offered by Edgemont Education Lending, including eligibility criteria and the process for requesting release.

The cosigner notice must be provided at or before the time the cosigner signs the loan agreement. Receipt of the notice must be acknowledged by the cosigner and documented in the loan file.
