# GOLD-17: Elevated DTI with Address Mismatch Decline

## Decision

**DECLINE** | Risk Grade: **D3** | Adverse Action Required: **Yes**

## Reasoning Chain

1. **Borrower eligibility**: Hugo Quintero, 30-year-old permanent resident, applying for GR loan at Harborview State University (Tier A). Cosigner present.

2. **Credit evaluation**: Borrower FICO 737 with established file. Clean credit history. Cosigner FICO 782 -- excellent. On credit scores alone, both parties would qualify.

3. **Income and DTI -- FAIL**: Borrower's own DTI of 49.5% exceeds the maximum ceiling. With gross annual income of only $36,410 ($3,034/month), monthly debt service of $1,500, and housing expense of $1,197, the borrower is overextended. RC-003 (excessive DTI) applies per POL-009.

4. **Cosigner strength insufficient**: While cosigner has excellent FICO 782 and lower DTI of 16.0%, the borrower's own DTI must still meet minimum thresholds under GR product rules. The cosigner can supplement but not fully substitute for borrower financial capacity per POL-004 EDU-COS-001.

5. **Fraud concern**: Address mismatch flag is triggered in fraud screening. While not a standalone decline reason, it adds to the risk profile and requires investigation per POL-012 EDU-FRAUD-001.

6. **School eligibility**: Harborview State University is Tier A with 4.1% CDR and 82% completion rate. The school is not the issue.

7. **Risk grade**: D3 reflects the DTI overage on the primary borrower despite cosigner strength.

## Policy Citations

- POL-002 EDU-UW-001 (underwriting standards -- borrower DTI limits)
- POL-009 RC-003 (excessive DTI)
- POL-012 EDU-FRAUD-001 (address mismatch investigation)

None. The issue is financial capacity, not documentation.

## Conditions

None. Hard decline. Borrower should consider reducing debt obligations or increasing income before reapplying.
