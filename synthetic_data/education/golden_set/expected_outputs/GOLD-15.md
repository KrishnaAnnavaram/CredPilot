# GOLD-15: Cosigner Adequacy Check on INTL Application

## Decision

**DECLINE** | Risk Grade: **D2** | Adverse Action Required: **Yes**

## Reasoning Chain

1. **Borrower eligibility**: Xander Aldridge, 23-year-old F-1 visa holder from Bangladesh. Applying for INTL product at Pacific Rim Business School (Tier B, Singapore).

2. **Immigration status**: Valid F-1 visa with I-94 verified and I-20 on file. STEM OPT eligible with 36 months post-study work authorization. These are positive INTL factors.

3. **Cosigner evaluation -- FAIL**: Cosigner Gavin Nakamura (parent, US citizen) provides the required US-based credit backing. FICO 712 meets minimum thresholds. However, the cosigner's DTI of 46.6% exceeds the maximum allowable ceiling of 43% per POL-004 EDU-COS-001. With gross income of $77,252 and monthly debt service of $2,998 plus housing of $2,177, the cosigner's debt load is unsustainable.

4. **RC-003 application**: The cosigner DTI of 46.6% triggers RC-003 (excessive debt-to-income ratio) per POL-009. Even though the cosigner citizenship status is valid (US citizen), the financial capacity test fails.

5. **Borrower limitations**: Borrower has NO_FILE credit status (expected for F-1) and no independent income verification. The INTL product relies heavily on cosigner financial strength, which is insufficient here.

6. **Residual income**: Cosigner residual income of $3,439/month after adding the proposed loan payment is marginal.

## Policy Citations

- POL-004 EDU-COS-001 (cosigner requirements -- DTI ceiling)
- POL-010 EDU-INTL-001 (international student underwriting)
- POL-009 RC-003 (excessive DTI)

## Documents Requested

None. The issue is the cosigner's financial capacity, not documentation.

## Conditions

None. Hard decline. Borrower should consider applying with a different cosigner who meets DTI requirements or reducing the loan amount to bring cosigner DTI within limits.
